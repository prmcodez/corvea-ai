RESPONSIBLE AI

Building healthcare AI responsibly.

Corvea is designed to explore the opportunities and limitations of computer vision in healthcare education.

Model uncertainty

AI results can be wrong. Corvea should communicate uncertainty rather than presenting guesses as facts.

Image quality

Lighting, focus, camera quality, and image angle can affect computer vision analysis.

Bias

AI systems must be evaluated for differences in performance across different image conditions and populations.

Human oversight

AI should support health education rather than replace qualified healthcare professionals.
